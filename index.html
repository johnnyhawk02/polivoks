WHAT IS MODELLED (component level)
VCO ×2KR198NT1A expo pair (Ebers–Moll with emitter-side series resistance from R4/R5 and the transistor, thermal drift of VT) sinking current from node X under the 5600 pF timing cap, which hangs from the A6 +10.24 V reference. Reset by the V1/V5 PNP–NPN latch, gated by V6/V7 and the R18/R19 divider, with A7 buffering the ramp; the cap swing, dump time and trigger lag come from an ngspice model of the core (spice/vco), which also sets the high-note flattening. Saw AC-coupled through C8/R29; A8 precision-rectifier triangle with the KD521 dead zone; A10 comparator pulse.
VCFTwo K140UD12 (uA776) programmable op-amps as OTA-C integrators: dV/dt = (Iset/C1)·tanh(Vd/4VT) with the 20 % slew asymmetry of the uA776, the exact resistor network at A2’s input (mixer 39k, LP 47k, 1k, resonance pot 100k + 47k), 47k/1k into A3, 2 mV offsets, rail clamping. Iset comes from A1/V1: Vbe = R2·(Iin − Ic/β), summing the cutoff, envelope and modulation pot currents; V1 saturates at ≈224 µA. Checked against a transistor-level ngspice model of the uA776 datasheet schematic (spice/ folder). Solved implicitly at 4× oversampling.
Envelopes ×25 µF capacitor charged and discharged through the pots and KD521 diodes (Shockley equation solved per sample); comparator ends attack at +6 V; repeat mode retriggers at the sustain level.
VCADifferential transistor pair: Itail·tanh(Vin/2VT) with control-voltage bleed from pair mismatch; output coupling capacitor.
ModulatorIntegrator + Schmitt trigger (triangle/square), sample-and-hold of the noise source, filtered noise.
KeyboardKey string of 47 × 100 Ω fed 1.25 mA (125 mV per key); one key bus, so two keys give the lower key directly and the upper one through A4 from the shorted string. A 16–23 kHz pilot detects a pressed key and opens the KP303V track-and-hold (≈0.4 ms to settle); with no key down both voices hold and voice 2 falls to voice 1. Glide (R48 1 MΩ, C4 0.22 µF) only on VCO I; the 1/2-voice switch gives VCO II the lower or the upper key. Legato does not retrigger the envelopes (spice/kybd).
VCO CV summerA2 virtual earth at 15 µA per octave: keyboard and registers through 100k, modulation pots through 47k (±2 octaves at full), Generator II FM through R64/R66/R63, detune through 1 MΩ (±10 semitones). Checked in spice/vco/summer.cir.
MixerOp-amp summer saturating at the ±12.5 V rails.
Estimated valuesC1 (≈25 pF, fitted to the datasheet slew rate and bandwidth), pot tapers, KT315G Is/β, op-amp excess phase at full resonance, KR140UD8 slew and output current, KR198NT1A bulk resistance and β, one wire junction at the VCO’s A7/V7 (see spice/vco/README), diode parameters and some pot ranges are estimates where the schematic scan is unreadable or the value is unpublished.
