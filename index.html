WHAT IS MODELLED (component level)
VCO ×2КР198НТ1А matched pair solved with Ebers–Moll + bulk emitter resistance (high-end flattening) and thermal drift of VT; 5600 pF timing cap charged by the collector current and integrated exactly per step; comparator + transistor reset with finite discharge time; precision-rectifier triangle; К140УД8 comparator pulse with 3 V/µs slew.
VCFTwo К140УД12 (uA776) programmable op-amps as OTA-C integrators: dV/dt = (Iset/C1)·tanh(Vd/4VT) with the 20 % slew asymmetry of the uA776, the exact resistor network at A2’s input (mixer 39k, LP 47k, 1k, resonance pot 100k + 47k), 47k/1k into A3, 2 mV offsets, rail clamping. Iset comes from A1/V1: Vbe = R2·(Iin − Ic/β), summing the cutoff, envelope and modulation pot currents; V1 saturates at ≈224 µA. Checked against a transistor-level ngspice model of the uA776 datasheet schematic (spice/ folder). Solved implicitly at 4× oversampling.
Envelopes ×25 µF capacitor charged and discharged through the pots and КД521 diodes (Shockley equation solved per sample); comparator ends attack at +6 V; repeat mode retriggers at the sustain level.
VCADifferential transistor pair: Itail·tanh(Vin/2VT) with control-voltage bleed from pair mismatch; output coupling capacitor.
ModulatorIntegrator + Schmitt trigger (triangle/square), sample-and-hold of the noise source, filtered noise.
Mixer / glideOp-amp summer saturating at the ±12.5 V rails; glide is an RC lag on each key CV.
